# Notice: chemical-risk example

## Authorship

The files in this folder (research brief, dossiers, instructional design, storyboards, interactive HTML course,
QA report, prompts and screenshots) were created by William Ebong as portfolio work and are included in the
CourseForge repository, under the repository's MIT licence, as a development and regression fixture.

## Content and sources

- The course content paraphrases and summarises public occupational health and safety guidance, legislation and
  technical references. Sources are cited in the dossiers and in the course's reference library.
- No standards text, regulation text or other copyrighted material is reproduced beyond short identifying
  references (titles, section numbers, citations). Protected figures and hazard pictograms are not reproduced.
- Legislative references for Alberta were located with a legislation search tool. Such tools, and any text or
  links taken from them, are not the official legal version of the legislation; the official versions are those
  published by the King's Printer.

## Not for operational use

This material is an example of course engineering, not safety advice or a compliance resource. Legislation,
standards and guidance change: re-check every source for currentness and jurisdiction before any operational,
training or compliance use, and have qualified people review the content.
