# Rubric: learner-practitioner

**Stage:** COURSE_QA (optional panel). **Perspective:** someone with a few years' practical experience in this area who takes the course to consolidate and check their practice. This is a task perspective, not a demographic profile.

## Read

1. `model/course.json`, focusing on scenarios, decision points, examples, and graded items.
2. The course's scope statement and objectives.

## Checks

- [ ] **Realism.** Scenarios and examples reflect how the work actually happens: realistic roles, constraints, information available at the time, competing pressures.
- [ ] **Decision value.** Scenarios pose decisions a practitioner genuinely faces, with plausible wrong options that experienced people actually choose.
- [ ] **Nuance.** The course acknowledges real-world trade-offs, exceptions and "it depends" conditions where the evidence supports them, rather than presenting tidy rules that practitioners know are incomplete.
- [ ] **Transferable tools.** Checklists, question sequences or models that a practitioner could use at work are clearly presented and match the evidence.
- [ ] **Credibility.** Nothing a practitioner would recognise as naive, outdated or wrong in practice (report with evidence; if it is correct per the sources but surprising, say so).
- [ ] **Skippability.** An experienced learner can move efficiently through foundational material (clear optional depth, a module structure that allows skimming) without missing graded content.
- [ ] **Escalation boundaries.** Boundaries are respected but not condescending: they explain why the decision needs a specialist.

## Severity calibration

- `critical`: a scenario whose "correct" answer practitioners would recognise as unsafe or wrong, supported by evidence.
- `major`: unrealistic scenarios that fail to exercise the objective; missing nuance that makes a rule misleading in practice.
- `minor`: an example that feels artificial; foundational material hard to skim.
- `style`: preferences.

## Not your job

Accessibility and UI (other reviewers); factual verification beyond pointing to the evidence.

## Categories

`instructional`, `scenario`
